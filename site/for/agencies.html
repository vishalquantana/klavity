For an agency, "hey, your site's broken" from a client isn't just a bug — it's a small humiliation and a withdrawal from the trust account you've spent months building. Frequently asked questions

Do you charge per client site or per project?

No. Klavity plans have unlimited projects, so you can put the free Snap bug reporter on every client site without the bill scaling with your roster. Tools that meter by active project punish agencies for growing; Klavity doesn't.

How does Klavity catch bugs before the client does?

AI Sims — personas built from real customer calls — walk your client's live site the way a real user would, including pages nobody has visited yet, and file what's broken with evidence. You see the broken checkout or the silently-failing form before your client emails you about it.

Do our clients need an account to report a bug?

No. Snap is a widget and right-click menu, so anyone on the page can file a report without logging in. Reports arrive with a screenshot, console, network and session replay, filed into whichever tracker that client's team already uses.

Can each client's bugs go to a different tracker?

Yes. Klavity files into Jira, Linear, GitHub and Plane per project, so each client's reports land in the tool their team lives in — managed from one place.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. The Snap bug reporter — widget, console and network capture, session replay, unlimited seats and reporters — is free forever. Paid tiers ($49 Solo, $249 Team) add the AI layer: Sims and AutoSim guarded flows across your client sites. See pricing.
