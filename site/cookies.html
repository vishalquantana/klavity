What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies — such as analytics scripts, pixels and local storage — work in comparable ways. We group everything we use into two buckets: essential and analytics.

Essential vs analytics

  • Essential. Needed for the site and app to work — for example, the session cookie that keeps you signed in to the Klavity dashboard. The app and dashboard use only essential session cookies. Essential cookies do not require your consent.
  • Analytics. Help us understand how the marketing site is used so we can improve it. These are not essential, and — where required by law — we load them on the basis of your consent. You can opt out at any time (see below).

How to opt out

  • Browser controls. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and block third-party scripts. Doing so switches off the analytics tools above; essential session cookies will still be set where needed for the app to function.
  • Provider opt-outs. Use the per-tool opt-out links in the table above.
  • Do Not Track / GPC. We honour browser-level tracking-preference signals where our tooling supports them.
